Registering Apps to Stop Scrolling In

You register the apps to stop scrolling in the Registration tab of the Settings window. To open the Settings window, choose Settings... from the menu of the menu bar icon or the Dock icon. Then click Registration in the toolbar.

Stop Scrolling In

Choose which apps to stop scrolling in.

All Apps
Scrolling is always stopped, no matter which app you’re using. The directions to stop follow Directions to Stop in the Scrolling tab.
Registered Apps
Scrolling is stopped only while an app in the Registered Apps list is frontmost. This is the default.
Note When an app that isn’t registered is frontmost, scrolling isn’t stopped in registered apps behind it.

While All Apps is selected, the Registered Apps list is dimmed and can’t be changed.

Registering apps

Use any of these methods:

  • Click the + button below the list, then choose an app from the menu. The menu lists running apps that aren’t registered yet.
  • From the + button’s menu, choose Choose from Applications Folder..., select the apps, then click Add. You can select several apps at once.
  • Drag apps from the Finder to the list.

When you register an app, its directions to stop start with the Directions to Stop setting in the Scrolling tab.

Removing apps

  1. Select the apps you want to remove in the list.
  2. Click the − button below the list, or press Delete.

Choosing the directions to stop for each app

Use the two buttons at the right end of each row in the list to choose the directions to stop for that app. Scrolling stops in the directions whose buttons are highlighted.

Left button (up and down arrows)
Stops vertical scrolling.
Right button (left and right arrows)
Stops horizontal scrolling.

Each click turns the highlight on or off. You can’t turn off both.
