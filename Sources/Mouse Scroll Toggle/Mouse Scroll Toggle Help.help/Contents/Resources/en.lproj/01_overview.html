About Mouse Scroll Toggle

What the app does

Mouse Scroll Toggle is a utility app that stops mouse scrolling. It runs in the menu bar or the Dock.

Register the apps you want to stop scrolling in, and scrolling stops only while one of those apps is frontmost.

Note When an app that isn’t registered is frontmost, scrolling isn’t stopped in registered apps behind it.
  • Stop Scrolling In: Choose whether to stop scrolling in all apps or only in registered apps.
  • Directions to Stop: Choose vertical only, horizontal only, or both. You can set this for each registered app.

To register apps, see Registering Apps to Stop Scrolling In. For other settings, see Settings.

Note Mouse Scroll Toggle needs permission to control your Mac to stop scrolling. For details, see Permissions and Troubleshooting.

Reading the icons

The icon shows whether scrolling is currently stopped.

Menu bar icon
A blue switch means you can scroll; a red switch means scrolling is stopped. Hold the pointer over the icon to see the current state in words. If permission to control your Mac is missing, the icon changes to a warning triangle.
Dock icon
While scrolling is stopped, the icon changes to a red icon that shows scrolling is stopped.

Menu

Click the menu bar icon, or Control-click the Dock icon, to open the menu.

Settings...
Opens the Settings window, where you register apps and choose the directions to stop.
Help
Opens this help.
Quit
Quits Mouse Scroll Toggle. When the app quits, scrolling is no longer stopped anywhere.

To choose whether the app appears in the menu bar, the Dock, or both, use Display Mode in Settings.
